# Fieldnotes Kit — sample workbook

Synthetic demonstration product. Use this sample to rehearse delivery.

## Before the interview

Who are you speaking with? What workflow do you want to understand? What evidence would change your current belief? Ask permission before recording or retaining personal information.

## Five prompts

1. Walk me through the last time you did this task.
2. Where did you get stuck?
3. What did you do next?
4. What have you already tried to improve it?
5. What happens if the problem stays unsolved?

## After the interview

Separate exact observations, your interpretation, and open questions. Note the date and permitted source location. Write one follow-up question. Do not turn one interview into a claim about the whole market.
