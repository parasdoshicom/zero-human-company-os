# Fictional business: Fieldnotes Kit

**Synthetic teaching example. No live offer, customer, or payment.**

Customer: independent consultants preparing their first customer-discovery interviews. Problem hypothesis: they jump to pitching before understanding the buyer's workflow. Product: a short interview workbook with a planning sheet, five prompts, and a debrief. Price hypothesis: $29 once. Promise: structure an interview and organize the notes; no promise of sales or validated demand.

Included: [the sample product](product.md), [local offer page](landing.html), and [synthetic events](ledger.json). The sample is intentionally small so the audience can inspect the entire path.

The simulated funnel has 120 qualified visits and zero offer interactions in a fixed window. That does not establish a universal conversion threshold. It provides one deterministic routing example: inspect the promise and handoff before building another product.

A real pilot would need customer research, a finished product, owner-approved public identity, checkout and delivery, terms, support/refund ownership, valid analytics, and a legitimate distribution channel. None is provisioned by the demo.
