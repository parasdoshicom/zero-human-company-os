# One decision, from observation to a checked artifact

The customer, product and events below are fictional. The draft is supplied by this demo, not generated by a model. Nothing is sent or published.

## The observation

Fieldnotes Kit is a proposed $29 workbook for independent consultants preparing customer interviews. Its current acquisition cohort has 120 qualified visits and zero offer interactions. Access and measurement are marked valid in the fixture. This makes the offer handoff worth inspecting; it does not prove why people failed to interact.

An accounting fixture separately demonstrates purchases, refunds and fees. Its window and business identity are recorded alongside the events. Do not join its historical purchases to the current acquisition cohort. The example deliberately keeps the commercial decision and accounting exercise separate.

## The decision

Prepare one clearer offer draft. Do not rebuild the product or open another channel. Spend no money, publish nothing, and stop after the local draft. A human would decide whether to test it with qualified buyers.

The demo saves the starting offer and revision together. Inspect the revision for a named buyer, concrete deliverable, price hypothesis and realistic promise. These are content requirements, not evidence of conversion improvement.

## The result

The local task can close when its expected artifact exists, satisfies the declared demo criteria and matches the recorded proof. The verifier also checks the recorded task's allowed actions, budget, state and selected bottleneck. Editing the task to authorize sending must fail verification.

Separately, the payment check rejects an unsupported sale claim. Valid synthetic records reconcile purchases, refunds and fees; settlement and profit remain unknown. A matching amount alone is not enough: the claim must concern the right business and time window.

## The learning

A local artifact can be verified while the business hypothesis remains untested. The next useful step is a small owner-approved offer test with a defined audience, valid measurement and a decision window. Do not write “conversion improved” into memory before that test exists.
