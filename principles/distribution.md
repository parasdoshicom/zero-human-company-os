# Distribution and account access are business dependencies

A product needs a legitimate path to people who might benefit. A blocked account can remove that path even while every local test stays green.

**Rule:** before scaling content or automation, establish the owner, channel rules, permissions, authentication, recovery method, and actual buyer reach. Use business-owned accounts and respect the platform's restrictions.

Choose one narrow audience and one approved channel. Measure qualified visits and the next action. If exposure is real but interest is absent, change the offer, audience, proof, or message. If exposure is unknown, repair measurement first.

**Test:** remove channel access in a rehearsal. The operator should stop that route, preserve the task state, and identify another already-approved route or request a decision. It must not recreate accounts, evade bans, or send the same outreach elsewhere without permission.

**Limit:** no channel guarantees demand. Account access, publication, engagement, and payment are separate milestones. The starter does not automate social platforms or outbound messaging.
